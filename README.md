Hey! Welcome to my personal website. My name is Jorge and I am a graduate in mathematics, currently looking for a PhD position. In 2025 I obtained my master's degree from Stockholm University and the KTH Royal Institute of Technology. I wrote my [master's thesis](./thesis_master.pdf) on the Halperin conjecture, an open problem in algebraic topology about the collapse of the Serre spectral sequence of certain fibrations. Before that, in 2023, I had obtained bachelor's degrees in mathematics and physics from the University of Cantabria. In addition, I obtained a master's degree in particle and cosmos physics from the University of Cantabria in 2026.
## Research interests
Briefly, I am interested in any problem involving the study of the topology of a geometric object, or a more abstract entity, e.g. groups, through some generalisation of the common tools used in topology. In my thesis about the Halperin conjecture, I studied the relation between the topological formulation of the conjecture and the algebraic statement, in terms of the derivations of the cohomology algebra of the fibre. During that process, I gained experience with graded structures, derivations and filtrations in the context of (rational) homotopy theory, and their role in representing the topology of the underlying spaces. Thus, I look forward to carry over this knowledge to conduct research in algebraic topology and its applications in the coming future.

More generally, the following is a list of topics about which I have recently been reading:
* Differential topology.
* Geometric group theory.
* Topological data analysis.
